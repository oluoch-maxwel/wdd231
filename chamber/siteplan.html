Student Travel Kenya

Affordable Adventures for Students

1. Site Name

Student Travel Kenya

The name Student Travel Kenya clearly identifies the purpose of the website. It focuses on helping students discover interesting and affordable places to visit across Kenya.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Student Travel Kenya is to help students discover affordable and interesting destinations across Kenya.

The website will provide information about destinations, including their counties, activities, entrance fees, estimated costs, locations, and useful travel tips.

JavaScript will be used to dynamically display destination information and allow users to filter destinations based on interests such as nature, wildlife, beaches, culture, hiking, and adventure.

3. Scenarios

Scenario 1

"Where can I go for a weekend trip in Kenya if I have a budget of KSh 2,000?"

Scenario 2

"Which places in Kenya can I visit if I am interested in nature and wildlife?"

Scenario 3

"What activities can I do at a destination, and how much is the entrance fee?"
