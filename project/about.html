About our website

Why We Created This Website

Student Travel Kenya was created to help students discover interesting and affordable places to visit across Kenya. We understand that travelling can be expensive, especially for students, so we provide reliable information to make trip planning easier and more affordable.

Our Purpose

This website provides information about different destinations, activities, entrance fees, and other useful details for students planning a trip. It also uses JavaScript to display destination information dynamically and help users explore destinations based on their interests.
